A LETTER, JUST FOR YOU19 September 2026

My dear Mee Mee,

I first noticed the quiet girl in the corner. You weren’t trying to get anyone’s attention, and somehow you had mine. Then you walked straight up to me. I never expected that small moment at immigration to lead to someone becoming this important to me.

We started with random conversations. Then the conversations became something I looked forward to. Coffee, a walk, a noisy night, a little time together. Ordinary memories started feeling special because you were in them. Even talking for hours felt easy.

But what really changed how I felt was the way you cared. “Did I make you mad, koko?” “Did I say something wrong, koko?” “Are you sad, koko?” I hear the worry behind those questions. You pay attention to how I feel, even when I haven’t found the words to explain it. I want you to feel safe enough to ask, and I want to get better at answering honestly and gently.

I notice when you remind me to get home safely. I notice that when I’m sweating after a walk, you’re already holding a tissue. You’re usually so particular about cleanliness, but when you’re taking care of me, you put that aside without making a big thing of it. Those moments make me feel safe. They make me feel cared for.

I don’t always say it in the moment, but none of those details are lost on me. I remember them. I remember you choosing to be kind, choosing to check on me, choosing to come a little closer.

Being with you makes me want to be more sincere, more serious, and better in the way I love. I want to listen properly, say what I mean, and give you the same care you give me. We’re still learning each other. I like that there is so much left to discover, and I want to keep finding out where this relationship goes with you.

For your birthday, I hope you feel seen and appreciated for the person you are. The quiet parts. The thoughtful parts. The particular parts. All the small things that make you, you.

Happy Birthday, Mee Mee.
I’m really glad you walked up to me.

Yours,
koko
